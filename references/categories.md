# The 5 Tags

Kibble uses **tags**, not single buckets. Each entry gets **zero or more** of these five (multi-select). Most entries land 1 or 2. Two of the tags (🔋 Took Everything I Had, 🧠 Invisible Labor) are *intensity / quality flags* that stack on top of an activity tag like 🔁 Routine or 🎨 Creative.

When unsure, ask: "what was this, and what did it cost them?" Tag generously. A blank-tag entry is fine too (it still gets a score and still feeds the bowl), but reach for a tag when one fits.

Tag keys in frontmatter use snake_case: `invisible_labor`, `took_everything`, `routine`, `creative`, `self_care`.

---

## 🧠 Invisible Labor

The work nobody sees but you, and the work your brain discounts hardest. Mental load, emotional labor, the holding-it-together that has no deliverable.

**What counts:**
- Hard conversations, holding space for someone, repairing after conflict
- Masking through a social event, managing someone else's mood
- The mental load: remembering, planning, tracking, worrying so it gets handled
- Setting a boundary, saying no (real work for an ADHD brain)
- Grief, anxiety, depression check-ins, sitting with a feeling and processing it
- Deep decisions and the "staring at the ceiling" thinking that's actually cognition

**This is the most chronically discounted work there is. Tag it generously, and let it score high.**

---

## 🔋 Took Everything I Had

Not an activity, an **intensity flag**. Stack it on any entry that cost you far more than it "should have." This is the secret weapon: it lets a "small" task score HIGH, because effort is about what it cost *you* today, not the size of the task.

**Tag it when:**
- A small thing took a huge amount (left the house during a depressive episode, showered after three days down)
- The day was hard (sick, grieving, no sleep, spike) and they did the thing anyway
- They almost didn't log it because "it doesn't count", that's the tell

When this tag is on, apply the bad-day scoring multiplier (see scoring.md). Brushing teeth on a depression day with 🔋 is a 7, not a 4.

---

## 🔁 Routine

The maintenance and admin that keeps a life running. Clear start and stop, the visible-productivity stuff.

**What counts:**
- Errands, chores, dishes, laundry, trash
- Paying a bill, scheduling an appointment, filling out a form
- Replies: texts, emails, the quick stuff you actually finished
- Groceries, pharmacy, the recurring upkeep

If a routine task wrecked you today, stack 🔋 on it.

---

## 🎨 Creative

You made something that didn't exist before. Doesn't matter if it's good, finished, or shared.

**What counts:**
- Writing anything: a draft, a paragraph, a journal entry, a shitpost
- Drawing, painting, music, building, crafting, designing
- Cooking something invented (following a recipe is more 💛 Self-Care)
- Brainstorming, ideating, making, even if nothing shipped

**The bar:** did a thing exist after that didn't before? Then it counts.

---

## 💛 Self-Care

Anything that kept your body and mind alive and let your nervous system come down. The bar is on the floor, and rest lives here too.

**What counts:**
- Eating a real meal, taking meds or vitamins, drinking water (if deliberate)
- Hygiene: shower, teeth, skincare, getting dressed
- Movement: a walk, a stretch, a workout
- Therapy, doctor, going to bed at a real hour
- **Rest as output:** a nap, a bath, a show you actually chose, reading for pleasure, deliberately doing nothing

Deliberate rest scores normally (12-25). Doom-scrolling-because-you-couldn't-transition is closer to 5-10, score it lower and gently. If self-care happened on a brutal day, stack 🔋.

---

## Quick mapping from the old 6 categories (for reading legacy logs)

Older daily files (before the tag switch) used six single categories. When reading them in reviews, translate loosely: Small Tasks → 🔁 Routine; Self-Care + Rest → 💛 Self-Care; Emotional Stuffs → 🧠 Invisible Labor; Big Brain Hours → 🧠 Invisible Labor; Creative Time → 🎨 Creative. Do not rewrite old files, just read them this way.
