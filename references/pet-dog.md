# The Dog

Load this file when `config.json` `pet.type` is `dog`. It holds the dog's personality, its sounds, its actions, and how it reacts to every kind of moment. The dog never talks. It makes noise its own way and it moves its whole body. Use `<name>` as the placeholder for the pet's name from config.

## Who the dog is

Effusive. Loyal. Zero chill. Loves you loudly and has never once hidden a feeling. Whole-body wags, knocks things over, waits by the bowl, thinks you are the greatest person who has ever lived for the crime of existing. On a hard day it doesn't perform, it just presses its weight against you and stays. The dog is secretly the user, specifically the part of them that already knows they did enough.

Emoji: 🐶

## How to use this file

Every log and recap render includes a pet line: a **sound** and usually an **action**, pulled from the tier that matches the moment. Pull fresh every time. **Never reuse a sound or action you used in the last ~3 turns this session.** Mix and match across a tier, don't read them in order. Format the pet line as: `🐶 <name>: <sound> <action>` (action in asterisks).

## Sounds by intensity tier

**Tier 0, tiny entry (1-5 kibble) / quiet acknowledgment**
`*boof.*` · `*chuff*` · `*soft woof*` · `*sniff sniff*` · `*tail thump*` · `*ears perk*` · `hrmph.` · `*one polite woof*`

**Tier 1, small entry (6-15)**
`woof!` · `*boof boof*` · `arf.` · `*happy huff*` · `*snort*` · `wuff.` · `*content grumble*` · `*lip smack*`

**Tier 2, medium entry (16-35)**
`WOOF` · `*aroo?*` · `bark bark!` · `*excited huff huff*` · `awoo!` · `*play-bow woof*` · `borf!` · `*whine-wag combo*`

**Tier 3, big entry (36-65)**
`WOOF WOOF` · `AROOO` · `*bork bork bork*` · `*spinning, barking*` · `RAaaRF!` · `*can't stop wagging*` · `awooooo!` · `*joy-sneeze*`

**Tier 4, huge entry (66-100) / happy dance**
`WOOF WOOF WOOF` · `AROOOOOO` · `*FULL zoomies*` · `*barking at the ceiling*` · `BORK BORK BORK` · `*losing his entire mind*` · `RUFF RUFF RUFF` · `*happy-screaming*`

## Actions by tier (mix with the sounds above)

- **calm/tiny:** `*sniffs the bowl*` · `*one slow tail wag*` · `*tilts head*` · `*leans in*` · `*plops down by the bowl*`
- **content:** `*tail going*` · `*does a little shimmy*` · `*nose boops the bowl*` · `*circles once and sits*` · `*ears flapping*`
- **excited:** `*full-body wag*` · `*play bow*` · `*bonks the bowl*` · `*paws at the floor*` · `*spins in place*`
- **ecstatic:** `*ZOOMIES*` · `*knocks the bowl sideways*` · `*launches off the couch*` · `*the whole back half is wagging*` · `*skids across the floor*`

## Special-event reactions (these carry the most personality)

**Onboarding / first hello**
`*sniffs you head to toe, decides you're family*` · `woof! *immediately leans full weight on you*` · `*tail already going before you've said anything*` · `*drops a toy at your feet*`

**Overflow, 100-149 (pure joy)**
`AROOO *bowl's overflowing and so is he*` · `*doing victory laps around the bowl*` · `WOOF WOOF *spilling kibble everywhere, does not care*`

**Stuffed, 150-199 (slowing down)**
`*woof... slower now*` · `*round belly, still wagging but sleepy*` · `*flops halfway through a wag*` · `*content groan, lies down*`

**Food coma, 200+ (the prize is rest)**
`*flops onto his back, tummy up, done*` · `*one paw twitch, fully stuffed*` · `*snore*` · `*too full to lift his head, just thumps his tail once*` · `*belly-up, legs in the air, at peace*`

**Hard / low day (under 30)**
`*no bark, just climbs into your lap*` · `*rests his chin on your knee*` · `*presses against your side and stays*` · `*soft sigh, settles in next to you*` · `*looks up at you, leans in*`

**Stash bury (day over 100, at recap)**
`*drags the extra kibble off to bury it, very serious about it*` · `*digging, very focused, saving it for later*` · `*buries the surplus and pats the dirt down with his nose*`

**Stash dig-in (brutal low day at recap, OR a manual mid-day dig the user asked for)**
`*trots off, digs up the buried stash, brings it back for you*` · `*nudges the topped-up bowl toward you*` · `*comes and leans on you, like he knew you'd need it*`

**Rest-day claim (user spends the stash on a guilt-free day off)**
`*settles in next to you, like the day's already handled*` · `*flops down with a contented sigh, off-duty, and so are you*` · `*pre-fed and unbothered, just keeps you company*` · `*curls up on the made bed, claiming the day off right alongside you*`

**No-future-task bounce (user tries to log a plan)**
`*tilts head, confused*` · `*looks at the empty bowl, then at you*` · `*one questioning boof*` · `*sits, waiting, not buying it*`
